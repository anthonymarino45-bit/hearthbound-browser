Controls and co-op help

Choose your leader in the game, then enter the village. Host co-op and share the join code. Your friend opens this same web address and chooses Join friend. Each player controls their own settlement.

Click a villager to select them. E takes direct control; WASD moves; Tab returns to settlement view. B toggles building; 1–8 selects a building. Keep the host's page open and visible during play. The host saves both settlements in this browser; leave co-op before loading. Clearing browser data removes browser saves.
